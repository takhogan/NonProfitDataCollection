# Nighttime lights for Venezuela outage corroboration

Research checked 7 November 2026. These are planning notes, not a tested integration. Read the existing platform plan and component architecture. No accounts were created, providers contacted, services purchased, or authenticated data downloads performed.

## Which products are usable and actually current?

### Takeaway
NASA Black Marble is the best open nighttime-light starting point, but a new live integration must use NOAA-20/NOAA-21 products rather than Suomi-NPP. It can corroborate broad nighttime disruption; it cannot determine household electricity or aid eligibility.

### Cited Findings
- **Critical current change:** NOAA confirms that S-NPP distribution and high-rate broadcast stopped on **2 November 2026 at 15:40 UTC**, permanently. It directs users to NOAA-21 and NOAA-20. Thus VNP46 historical data remain useful, but VNP46 must not be proposed as a continuing live feed. [NOAA final confirmation, 2 November 2026](https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/news/update-4-cessation-of-suomi-national-polar-orbiting-partnership-s-npp-data-users-november-02-2026-1540-utc-and-decommissioning-issued-november-2-2026-1800-utc).
- NASA's standard Black Marble suite distinguishes A1 top-of-atmosphere radiance, A2 corrected daily nighttime radiance, A3 monthly, and A4 annual products. Grid spacing is 15 arc-seconds, approximately 500 m at the equator. Standard Collection 2 historical records start 19 January 2012 for S-NPP and 5 January 2018 for NOAA-20. These global products include Venezuela, subject to quality and acquisition gaps. [LAADS suite documentation](https://ladsweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/missions-and-measurements/science-domain/nighttime-lights).
- **Verified public catalog presence:** LANCE's Collection 5200 directory lists `VJ146A1_NRT`, `VJ146A2_NRT`, `VJ246A1_NRT`, and `VJ246A2_NRT`. NOAA-20 uses VJ146; NOAA-21 uses VJ246. This establishes published product endpoints, not successful local acquisition or a guaranteed service level. [LANCE directory](https://nrt3.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/archive/allData/5200/).
- NOAA-21 A2 NRT has a public NASA catalog record, DOI `10.5067/VIIRS/VJ246A2_NRT.002`, a global extent, HDF-EOS5 format, and an archive endpoint. Its catalog temporal metadata is only 15 July–26 October 2026 despite a 3 November update; do not treat that stale endpoint as a demonstrated live availability limit. [NASA catalog](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/viirs-jpss2-gap-filled-lunar-brdf-adjusted-nighttime-lights-daily-l3-global-15-arc-second-); [NOAA-21 archive directory opened successfully](https://nrt3.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/archive/allData/5200/VJ246A2_NRT/).
- Black Marble scientific downloads are free; NASA's general policy makes NASA-led mission data CC0 unless specially marked. The particular NOAA-21 catalog also identifies public access and government-works licensing. Preserve the product DOI, version, attribution and original metadata. These are open public data, not a nonprofit-only grant. [NASA training confirmation of free downloads](https://appliedsciences.nasa.gov/sites/default/files/Black%20Marble%20Q%26A%20Doc.pdf); [NASA science data licensing](https://science.data.nasa.gov/about/license); [NOAA-21 catalog](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/viirs-jpss2-gap-filled-lunar-brdf-adjusted-nighttime-lights-daily-l3-global-15-arc-second-).
- Authenticated archive downloads use an Earthdata Download bearer token. LAADS offers a JSON/REST API v2 with OpenAPI specifications. Its current alerts include retirement of the Classic API; avoid building against obsolete tutorials. [Download instructions](https://nrt3.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/archive/allData/5200/VJ246A2_NRT/); [API v2](https://ladsweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/tools-and-services/api-v2/specs/); [LAADS alerts](https://ladsweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/alerts-and-issues).
- NASA's 2024 user guide describes about three-hour NRT latency, but that is a documented nominal capability, not measured delivery for the pilot. A standard VNP46A2 archive example for observation date 1 September 2026 shows production/public file timestamps on 9 September; the NRT example for 27 October shows file modification on 28 October. Publication/modification timestamps can reflect reprocessing, so neither is a complete latency measurement. Do not promise every daily corrected tile within three hours. [User guide](https://ladsweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/api/v2/content/archives/Document%20Archive/Science%20Data%20Product%20Documentation/Black-Marble_v2.0_UG_2024.pdf); [standard archive example](https://ladsweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/archive/allData/5200/VNP46A2/2026/244/); [NRT archive example](https://nrt3.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/archive/allData/5200/VNP46A2_NRT/2026/300/).
- NASA documents real outages and calibration failures. In 2024 a calibration-coefficient error caused a spurious fall in Black Marble radiance and required reprocessing. Collection 1 stopped in January 2025. A naive light-drop detector could interpret an instrument problem as a power crisis. [NASA known issues](https://landweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/knownissue?as=5200&sensor=VIIRS); [S-NPP outage history](https://modaps.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/services/production/outages_suomi_npp.html).

### Inferences
- Start retrospective experiments with Collection 2 NOAA-20 A2 so the baseline and forward stream share a sensor. Add NOAA-21 only after measuring cross-sensor offsets. Retain S-NPP for longer historical context, with explicit sensor transition boundaries.
- Public imagery/data access does not make the workflow costless: analyst time, downloads, disk, processing, cloud execution/egress and maintenance still cost resources. A small area processed locally is preferable to paid hosting for an initial experiment.
- Treat daily sampling, valid clear-sky sampling, product processing delay, publication delay, polling delay and human review delay as distinct quantities. An outage occurring and resolving between nighttime passes can be invisible even with perfect processing.

### Gaps
- No authenticated retrieval, Venezuela tile completeness test, observed latency percentiles, local cloud-free availability estimate, outage-detection accuracy estimate or uptime SLA was verified. The forward product catalog/endpoints are verified; a functioning authenticated adapter is not.
- NOAA-20 deep archive paths and NOAA-21 recent-file listing were inaccessible through this browsing tool. That is a research-tool access limitation, not proof that NASA services are down.
- The exact usable start and retention duration of each NRT stream must be checked during a separately authorized proof of concept. Use standard archives for reproducible history rather than presuming NRT retention.

## How do alternatives compare, and what can lights establish?

### Takeaway
EOG composites and NASA's blue/yellow imagery can aid historical and visual interpretation. They do not provide independent confirmation of NASA Black Marble when derived from the same VIIRS acquisition, and broad lighting trends must not be converted into household service facts.

### Cited Findings
- EOG monthly and annual VNL products use 15 arc-second grids covering 75°N–65°S, including Venezuela, with a series beginning in 2012. Monthly files carry cloud-free coverage counts; EOG explicitly warns that zero radiance can reflect lack of good observations, particularly in the tropics. It also offers nightly radiance/cloud mosaics, updated as new imagery arrives, without a numerical delivery guarantee on that page. Many products are under CC BY 4.0, but the linked product-specific license document returned 404 during this review, so exact per-product scope needs verification before redistribution. [EOG VNL documentation and downloads](https://eogdata.mines.edu/products/vnl/).
- EOG Nightfire is a separate combustion/heat product, not a free substitute for nighttime electrical-service monitoring. Since January 2025 its data require a Data Use License; nonprofits have an annual subscription agreement. Do not assume all EOG products are free because some VNL composites are open. [EOG Nightfire](https://eogdata.mines.edu/products/vnf/); [nonprofit licensing](https://payneinstitute.mines.edu/viirs-nightfire-licensing/).
- NASA's blue/yellow product combines DNB and longwave infrared to make clouds easier to distinguish from potential outages. It is useful for a reviewer inspecting a reported broad event; it is not a calibrated per-household outage classification. [NASA explanation](https://disasters.nasa.gov/our-impact/news/new-black-marble-product-allows-quicker-detection-power-outages-wake-disasters).
- Black Marble's gap-filled values can come from an earlier high-quality observation or monthly climatology. The guide supplies quality and latest-retrieval information specifically so users can distinguish current and historical retrievals. A visually complete raster is not evidence of current conditions. [Collection 2 guide, section 5.4](https://ladsweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/api/v2/content/archives/Document%20Archive/Science%20Data%20Product%20Documentation/Black-Marble_v2.0_UG_2024.pdf).
- NASA's humanitarian-oriented resource describes the scale as neighborhood patterns rather than individual buildings; clouds, fires and seasonal effects complicate daily analysis. It recommends NOAA-20/21 for new work and cautions that dimming has multiple explanations. [NASA Lifelines](https://nasalifelines.org/hfs-data-series/human-rights/).
- The World Bank maintains free Python/R tools for Black Marble retrieval and zonal statistics. Its API documentation exposes quality filtering and warns that tiles can be missing. Verify supported product IDs before relying on a wrapper for NOAA-20/21; examples defaulting to VNP46 can now silently target a discontinued sensor. [BlackMarblePy documentation](https://worldbank.github.io/blackmarblepy/api/blackmarble.html); [World Bank R repository](https://github.com/worldbank/blackmarbler).

### Inferences
- Prioritize A2 corrected **observed** radiance for analysis, retaining A1/cloud context for review. Never count gap-filled brightness as evidence of electricity restoration. Never convert missing/poor QA into zero light.
- A sustained, quality-controlled drop over an ordinarily lit urban area can support an area-level report of disruption; it does not identify the cause. Streetlights, commercial activity, generators, lighting schedules, vegetation, industrial flares, sensor geometry and changes in population can affect the signal. Lit surroundings do not prove a household has electricity.
- A 500 m grid cell overlaps many households and potentially several circuits. Do not attach its area-level assessment as a finding about an individual home's supply. A displayed 30 m downscaled map would not create independent 30 m electricity measurements.
- Rural low-baseline areas are a structural blind spot. Compare only adequately lit baseline pixels and explicitly show coverage; do not let better-measured urban districts monopolize aid or reviewers' attention.
- EOG, NASA Black Marble, and Worldview may be three presentations of the same sensor observation. Store a common acquisition/source lineage and do not count them as three independent witnesses.
- Satellite lights cannot verify piped-water delivery, water potability, remaining household drinking supply, ability to reach a charging site, or individual assistance need. These require the existing service-specific intake and human/field evidence.

### Gaps
- EOG's exact current nightly access eligibility, price, API guarantees and redistribution terms were not established; exclude it as a dependency of the low-cost pilot until clarified without assuming CC BY covers everything.
- No statistical relationship between pixel dimming and Venezuela household service failure was validated. A universal percent-drop threshold would be unsupported.

## What is a safe low-cost proof of concept and integration?

### Takeaway
Add an optional external-evidence layer after operational intake/fulfillment. Run it in shadow mode and measure whether it improves human area-level understanding, never whether it can reject households.

### Cited Findings
- The existing local plan already separates `observation`, `evidence`, `source_group_id`, `review_decision`, `review_evidence`, `need` and `assistance_decision`; the components specify a worker for comparison jobs, shared domain rules and PostgreSQL/PostGIS, with no new analytics infrastructure. [Local platform plan](../../PLATFORM_PLAN.md); [local component plan](../../components/README.md).

### Inferences
- Proposed experiment: select two or three NGO pilot communities spanning a bright urban and a dimmer/peripheral area; use only aggregate boundaries. Build 6–12 months of NOAA-20 corrected observed-radiance baseline, then monitor 4–8 weeks in shadow mode. These are suggested study sizes, not performance claims or established sufficient sample sizes. Extend historical windows to cover wet/dry season where relevant.
- Compare baseline matched by season and observation conditions; report median and spread, usable-pixel fraction, observation count and time since last valid observation. Pre-register any anomaly rule and evaluate on held-out dates and areas. Exclude flares/industrial sites and unstable/near-zero baseline pixels or analyze them separately.
- Validation labels should be independent timestamped volunteer/charging-site checks or verified utility outage/restoration records, not the same community reports used to create candidate alerts. Record reference uncertainty and selection bias. Include no-outage periods, cloudy periods, short daytime outages, partial/local outages and dark rural communities.
- Measure fraction of area-days assessable, acquisition-to-first-download median/p90, false alerts per area-week, precision/recall only on independently labeled **observable** events, missed events by class, analyst minutes per useful alert and cost per monitored area-month. Publish the denominator and uncertainty; do not calculate household accuracy from area pixels. Also report all events, including unobservable ones, to expose practical coverage limits.
- Suggested engineering gates: every derived signal retains source ID/version/acquisition interval/QA; replay ingestion is idempotent; stale or missing product becomes `not_assessable`; simulated source outage never changes an aid decision; every signal can be reproduced from its source and rule version; a reviewer can explain why it supports only an area-level claim. Numeric usefulness thresholds belong with the NGO after baseline measurement.
- Worker: scheduled source adapter, checksum/version cache, retries/backoff, freshness alarms, QA decoding, clipping/zonal summary and revision handling. Use existing worker orchestration, not a new microservice. Domain: typed comparable-evidence rule that rejects mismatched time/scope/service. API/web: evidence cards with source link, acquisition and retrieval times, geographic overlap, coverage, caveats and reviewer disposition. Raw rasters live outside case rows; no household coordinates or WhatsApp content sent upstream.
- Suggested new logical records: `external_source` (provider/product/version/license/terms URL/lineage/update policy), `external_asset` (granule ID/acquisition interval/published_at/first_seen_at/retrieved_at/checksum/footprint/QA/source URL), `area_indicator` (area and boundary version/metric/unit/baseline interval/coverage/observed-vs-imputed/algorithm version/result interval/limitations), and `evidence_link` (external indicator→area observation or episode/relation/space and time overlap/rule version/reviewer). Preserve `organization_id` on restricted assessment and review links; public raster provenance may be shared reference data. Do not fabricate an `assessment` just to satisfy the current observation foreign key: create a distinct external observation path or explicit nullable-origin design with constraints.
- Human rules: same timestamp and area do not ensure same circuit; interpret no change as `no usable corroboration`, never `false report`; disagreements trigger optional clarification; urgency remains independent; external absence/conflict never automatically denies, downgrades, or closes aid. New/reprocessed data creates a revision rather than silently overwriting an earlier review basis.

### Gaps
- Pilot boundaries, NGO review capacity and independent field-reference data have not been supplied. No integration implementation is authorized by this research task, and no cloud-account costs or performance are assumed.
