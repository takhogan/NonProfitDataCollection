# NGO programs, non-satellite corroboration and general reliability

Research checked 7 November 2026. Planning only; no accounts, purchases, provider contacts or integrations. Read PLATFORM_PLAN.md and components/README.md. Recommendations below are design judgments, not claims of deployed capability. Websites and metadata were checked; no authenticated API, image order or production latency benchmark was performed.

## Which NGO programs are genuinely accessible, and on what terms?

### Takeaway
Treat free open source data, a free processing allowance, discounted commercial licenses, and event-triggered disaster access as four different things. The strongest low-cost route is open imagery with small local processing jobs; Earth Engine is an optional eligible processing platform, while Planet and disaster programs are later options rather than pilot dependencies.

### Cited Findings
- **Earth Engine noncommercial processing:** current documentation states quotas began April 27, 2026, with rollout still gradual. Verified noncommercial projects get Community 150 EECU-hours/month; Contributor 1,000 requires an active billing account but noncommercial Earth Engine itself is not charged; other Google Cloud services may be. Partner 100,000 requires a separate application tied to climate mitigation/adaptation/protection, impact evidence, and potentially weeks for approval. Exhaustion reduces throughput/concurrency rather than necessarily stopping tasks. These are computation units, not elapsed runtime. This is processing access, not a license overriding each dataset's terms. See [Google tier documentation](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/guides/noncommercial_tiers) and [noncommercial eligibility](https://earthengine.google.com/noncommercial/).
- **Planet NGO program:** discounted 12-month, noncommercial, **single-entity** bundles. Small: PlanetScope 120,000 km² and SkySat archive 5,000 km²; flexible tasking excluded unless added. Medium: PlanetScope 1.2 million km², SkySat archive 10,000 km², flexible tasking 1,250 km². Technical support included. Dollar price not published on this page. Single-entity terms matter for eventual multi-NGO sharing. [Planet nonprofit program](https://www.planet.com/nonprofit/).
- **Planet academic access is not general NGO access:** nonprofit/government employees are explicitly ineligible for E&R Basic; accredited-university applicants can apply. Basic is free, 3,000 km²/month with 30-day PlanetScope delay, personal research license; illustrative derivative publication with attribution is allowed, sale/value-added commercial tools are not. Do not suggest using an academic colleague's account as an NGO production workaround. [Planet education and research eligibility and terms](https://www.planet.com/industries/education-and-research/).
- Planet offers data search/access/delivery/tasking APIs, but the presence of an API does not establish entitlement to particular Venezuela scenes or delivery latency. [Planet contact/access overview](https://www.planet.com/contact-sales/?industry=education-and-research).
- **Vantor, formerly Maxar, Open Data:** current product documentation directs users to disaster event imagery through Discover and links its activation protocol. The linked protocol says releases are selected by an internal committee, normally major sudden-onset events, with COG pre/post imagery and metadata. It specifies CC BY-NC 4.0 (attribution, license link, change indication, noncommercial use; an OSM exception) and free public event releases. It is a 2020 protocol still linked from current docs; re-check each event's actual license. These are occasional released disaster footprints, not an unrestricted global archive or guaranteed Venezuela monitoring. [Current Vantor documentation](https://xpress-docs.maxar.com/OpenData/OpenData_intro.htm); [linked activation protocol](https://maxar-marketing.s3.amazonaws.com/files/downloads/119757_opendataprotocol_2020_04.pdf).
- **International Charter:** direct access ordinarily requires a national disaster management authority or delegated agency with map-use capacity and English request capability. Venezuela appears on the August 2026 authorized-country list. UN mechanisms can support UN relief agencies. An independent local NGO is not automatically an Authorized User. This is emergency-response activation with coordinated acquisition and mapping, not a scheduled monitoring API. [Universal Access](https://upload.disasterscharter.org/universal-access), [Authorized User criteria](https://download.disasterscharter.org/become-au), [activation workflow](https://disasterscharter.org/charter-activation-process).

### Inferences
- Rank Earth Engine after local processing for the POC: local jobs avoid a new eligibility/billing dependency. If already approved, Community may suffice for a few small areas, but measure EECU usage; do not promise sufficient quota before testing. Budget labor, bandwidth, disks, storage/egress and recurring operations even when imagery has zero license price.
- Planet optical images can contextualize surface water or physical damage; they cannot see electricity inside a house or establish piped water delivery/safety. A finer pixel is not a household service sensor. Cloud cover and acquisition timing remain limitations.
- NGO licenses need written verification of raw imagery redistribution, derived statistics/maps, multi-entity staff access, vendor hosting, retention after license expiry, Venezuela ordering restrictions and processing/egress charges before a later purchase. Approval for one NGO does not grant every future platform tenant access.
- For Vantor and Charter, examine existing released products when a major emergency occurs; do not build routine outage detection on activation approval. No fixed acquisition latency, scene resolution or Venezuela historical inventory was verified here; treat these as per-product metadata, not provider-wide constants.

### Gaps
No NGO-specific dollar quote, guaranteed Venezuela tasking/coverage, API entitlement, processing allowance or redistribution permission beyond published summary terms was established for Planet. No current Venezuelan Vantor event inventory was established; main program page was inaccessible to the web tool. Charter downstream imagery licenses and a guaranteed delivery SLA were not established. NASA CSDA and ESA third-party research access were not researched sufficiently to recommend them for this NGO's operational use; do not assume nonprofit status creates eligibility.

## Which non-satellite sources can corroborate Venezuela conditions?

### Takeaway
Manual field checks and authenticated utility notices aligned to service zones are the most operationally useful additions. Independent monitoring and humanitarian reports provide broader context. Internet telemetry is a useful cross-sensor clue for large disruptions, never proof of electricity supply to a particular household.

### Cited Findings
- **IODA:** public near-real-time Internet disruption monitoring at country, subnational and autonomous-system levels; its Venezuela dashboard exposes active-probing, BGP and network-telescope series, including unknown/invalid geographic labels. Its documented v2 API includes raw signals, metadata entities and outage alerts/events/summaries. [About IODA](https://ioda.inetintel.cc.gatech.edu/about/), [Venezuela dashboard](https://ioda.inetintel.cc.gatech.edu/country/VE?view=view2), [API](https://api.ioda.inetintel.cc.gatech.edu/v2/).
- **Cloudflare Radar:** outage records include location, network, scope, start/end time and likely cause, where the cause can be informed by external public reporting. API requests require a token. The data reflect Cloudflare's network and aggregated DNS observations. Download/API data are CC BY-NC 4.0; embeddable/downloadable graphs and images are CC BY 4.0. [Outage API guide](https://developers.cloudflare.com/radar/investigate/outages/), [licensing and data sources](https://radar.cloudflare.com/about).
- Venezuela coverage is concrete: Cloudflare documented an 82% country-level traffic decline during the August 30, 2024 power outage, beginning around 04:45 local time, followed by recovery. This demonstrates an area-level association in a large event, not a calibrated household detector. [Cloudflare's own disruption analysis](https://blog.cloudflare.com/q3-2024-internet-disruption-summary/).
- **CEDICE service monitoring:** describes monthly reports drawing from press, social media and citizen testimony. The inspected page exposes 2020–2024 archives, not a verified current daily feed. It states copyright reserved. Useful for historical patterns and methodology, but reports may reuse the same community claims the platform receives. [CEDICE methodology and archive](https://cedice.org.ve/ogp/gasto-publico/servicios/).
- **UNDP Venezuela:** a published methodology uses Twitter reports to identify service failures and municipality/state location; the described implementation covered a quarter. This is evidence that a method exists, not a verified continuously running open operational API. [UNDP service-indicator methodology](https://www.undp.org/es/venezuela/publicaciones/indicadores-de-calidad-de-servicios-basicos-un-enfoque-en-twitter).
- **Humanitarian reference data:** OCHA CODs provide administrative boundaries, population statistics, place names and P-codes for consistent geographic work, generally sourced from national authorities and accepted through country/regional information-management processes. The current status page shows Venezuela COD-AB vintage 2021, COD-PS 2011, and an ADM2 P-code join mismatch. This is a concrete reason to retain versions and inspect join failures instead of assuming matching names/codes. [OCHA COD overview](https://ocha-dap.github.io/cod-site/), [status dashboard](https://ocha-dap.github.io/cod-site/status/).
- **ReliefWeb:** v2 HTTPS JSON API supports humanitarian reports and related metadata; every request now requires a pre-approved `appname`. Approval involves a form and email, which this task did not undertake. Source organizations retain rights in material. Curated reports are contextual evidence, not uniformly licensed raw datasets or real-time service telemetry. [Current ReliefWeb API documentation](https://apidoc.reliefweb.int/), [read/search details](https://apidoc.reliefweb.int/read/).

### Inferences
**Practical order:** (1) NGO-managed fixed sentinel checks at charging sites and agreed community points, plus manually authenticated utility notices; (2) IODA/Cloudflare area disruption clues; (3) HDX/COD reference geography and ReliefWeb contextual reports; (4) CEDICE/other survey archives for baselines and sampling context. Satellite ranking belongs with other researchers' notes.

A utility notice establishes that the utility announced a planned interruption or asserted restoration. It does not prove the actual condition at each home. Record publisher identity and original URL, notice publication time, proposed versus actual event time, named circuits/pumping systems/places, canceled/revised notices, and a human service-zone mapping. A screenshot or forwarded announcement needs original-source confirmation; never infer authenticity from a logo or AI image detector. Water-system recovery may lag pump restart. The operator's claim of cause should be stored as attributed text rather than established fact.

IODA and Cloudflare can be sampled without sending household coordinates or phone numbers. Use public area codes. An ISP territory is not an electrical feeder; IP geolocation is approximate. Telecom-only failures, policy disruptions, generator-backed routers, mobile switching, daily usage changes and reporting-channel outages confound the relationship. Raw telemetry can be independent from resident testimony, but published causal annotations may reuse those same residents or media. Track those two provenance paths separately. Silence from WhatsApp during an outage is missingness, not restoration.

For all public sources, distinguish nominal publication cadence from actual observed latency. Public dashboard availability does not imply an uptime guarantee. Cache permitted metadata and links, retain last-successful retrieval, mark stale/missing distinctly, and allow manual evidence entry while a source is down. Historical range, geographic completeness and per-endpoint license must be checked before automated ingestion. A free public page is not a general right to republish its contents.

### Gaps
No trustworthy currently accessible official Corpoelec/Hidrocapital/Hidroven operational feed, public API, fixed publication cadence, complete outage archive or redistribution license was verified. Searches returned secondary reports and one unverified intranet-like domain that was excluded. This is an access-verification gap, not proof that official notices do not exist. The NGO should identify and authenticate local official channels during operational design; no provider was contacted. OVSP and VE Sin Filtro were not sufficiently checked for current methodological/API/license details to endorse integration here. IODA's exact delay, oldest available history, license text and SLA were not established; Cloudflare annotation latency and historic retention also need measurement. HDX Venezuela dataset page returned 403 to this tool, so resource-level license and current file contents were not independently confirmed. Do not assume all HDX datasets share one license.

## How should the platform maintain data reliability end to end?

### Takeaway
Reliability is a maintained operating process, not a numerical truth score or a satellite cross-check. Separate accurate observations, representative coverage, timely information, and correct fulfillment accounting; assign owners and monitor all four.

### Cited Findings
- UK Government's data-quality framework distinguishes completeness, uniqueness, consistency, timeliness, validity and accuracy, recommends identifying critical fields, measuring quality over time, recording ownership/metadata and fixing root causes. Different uses require different quality thresholds; correct syntax alone does not establish real-world accuracy. [Government data-quality guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-government-data-quality-framework/the-government-data-quality-framework-guidance).
- HDX manually reviews new datasets for relevance, timeliness, interpretability and comparability. This supports a review model that checks fitness for use rather than treating publication as truth. [HDX quality process](https://centre.humdata.org/ufaqs/how-does-hdx-ensure-data-quality/).
- OCHA describes humanitarian data responsibility as safe, ethical and effective management, including non-personal data; highlights disclosure risk and information-sharing protocols. Its guidelines were updated January 2025. [OCHA responsibility guidance](https://centre.humdata.org/data-responsibility/).

### Inferences
The following are proposed design additions, informed by the project plan and principles above, not externally verified NGO procedures:

**Definitions and intake.** Define electricity availability, intermittency, piped-water flow and household drinking-water reserves separately. Pilot Spanish questions with residents. Accept unknown, approximate and not-applicable explicitly; do not fill missing facts with defaults. Confirm the structured summary and provide a correction route. Preserve reported time/precision, received time, local timezone and first/secondhand scope. Keep a human-approved translation/extraction trail. Absence of a complete address or photo does not invalidate need.

**Identity and duplication.** Distinguish retry deduplication from entity resolution. Deterministically ignore a repeated provider message ID or fulfillment operation ID, but only suggest household merges. Shared phone, similar name or nearby point cannot establish one household. Keep reversible merge/split records and legitimate recurring need periods. Geographic proximity does not prove shared infrastructure.

**Provenance and independence.** Retain original resident assessment and normalized revision, with changed fields, actor, reason and supersession link. An evidence graph records common upstream origin: a WhatsApp forward, press story and CEDICE summary quoting it are one origin, not three independent confirmations. Two products using the same satellite granule are correlated. Give each relationship a claim, time/area compatibility check, reason, reviewer and rule version. A source being institutionally independent does not establish measurement independence.

**Review and validation.** Human reviewers adjudicate a specific claim, not a resident's honesty. Triage urgent needs independently of evidentiary uncertainty. Use unresolved/not-comparable outcomes; preserve disagreement and allow corrections. Sample unflagged cases as well as suspicious cases, and include low-reporting areas/channels. Otherwise measured accuracy becomes biased toward what the system already notices. Have two reviewers independently label a small sample and reconcile differences; retain initial disagreement. Don't train and evaluate a detector on labels derived from the same source. No conflicting evidence or absent satellite signal can automatically deny aid.

**Fulfillment truth.** Distributor reports, actual quantity/session completion and recipient confirmation remain different events. Reconcile water inventory/reserved/delivered/returned quantities and charging capacity versus booked/completed/interrupted sessions. No response to follow-up means unknown confirmation, not failed or successful delivery. A restored service observation does not close an unmet need.

**Operational quality ownership.** Caseworkers own corrections and current needs; a designated data steward owns dictionaries, geographic aliases, quality incidents and provenance; an evidence reviewer owns assessments; operators own source freshness/retries; coordinators own fulfillment reconciliation. Set a brief daily stale/failed-job/urgent-case check and weekly quality review. Log issue severity, affected records, cause, repair and prevention; audit repair propagation to aggregates and exports. Periodically recheck licenses/source availability and schema changes. Manual fallback preserves event identifiers for later idempotent import.

**Metrics with denominators.** Record observation-time completeness separately from field validity; proportion mapped only to broad areas; lag from observation to submission and submission to review (median/p90); active-need last-confirmed age; raw ingestion failures/quarantine/replay counts; duplicate false-merge/split corrections; independently checked claim agreement and unresolved share; reviewer disagreement; recipient-confirmation rate with unreachable/declined separate; fulfillment reconciliation discrepancies; coverage by area, channel and participation barriers. Never report resident reports as population prevalence without a sampling design. A decline in reports can reflect power/connectivity loss. Do not reward staff simply for high corroboration rates or forced field completion.

**Schema fitting the existing design.** Existing `observation.assessment_id` is mandatory, so do not invent assessments/households for raster or utility records. Add separate `external_source` (provider, product, license/version, access basis, expected cadence, scope, owner), `source_fetch` (requested window, received_at, status, latency, cursor, checksum, retries), and `external_indicator` (source item/version, phenomenon, value/unit, footprint/CRS, resolution, observed interval, published_at, first_seen_at, quality flags, valid coverage fraction, method/baseline version, upstream lineage, artifact link/checksum, expires_at, supersedes). Indicator states distinguish missing, stale, obscured, insufficient baseline, normal and anomaly. Avoid encoding a proxy as `service_status = available`.

Add `indicator_observation_link(organization_id, indicator_id, observation_id, relation, spatial_overlap, temporal_overlap, comparability_reason, rule_version, reviewer_id?, reviewed_at?)` with relation values `context_only`, `supports_area_condition`, `potentially_conflicts`, `not_comparable`. Use separate typed links to outage episodes and review decisions rather than unchecked polymorphic IDs. Extend review evidence links so a review can cite an external indicator without making it a household observation. Public licensed indicators may be shared reference records; per-NGO links, derived household comparisons and review decisions remain organization-owned. Private/commercial indicators also need explicit access grants and license scope. Enforce tenant-scoped foreign keys and authorization on every relationship. An area-level support relation must never be automatically promoted to household verification.

**Components.** Put read-only source adapters and scheduled acquisition/normalization in worker composition; contracts validates external payloads and indicator states; database stores lineage and spatial/time indexes; domain owns comparability/review rules; API authorizes reads/reviews; web shows acquisition versus publication versus retrieval time, usable coverage, source licensing, links, explanation and unknown state. Keep remote imagery processing off the WhatsApp webhook path. Start manual evidence entry and batched area summaries; no new microservice required.

**POC quality gates (proposals, not guaranteed performance).** First require 100% of imported indicators to have source/product/version, time window, geography and rights metadata or an explicit unknown/quarantine reason; zero automatic aid denials; replay causes no additional cases/allocations/deliveries; all high-impact merges reversible; expired indicators shown stale. Over 4–6 weeks, gather independent timestamped field checks in selected communities and normal periods as well as reported outages. Measure usable-data fraction, latency distribution and review burden before setting detection targets. If evidence rarely arrives within the NGO's action window or costs more reviewer time than it saves, retain it for retrospective area analysis rather than operations.

### Gaps
No local NGO sampling frame, service-zone boundaries, agreed response SLA, reviewer capacity, field validation set or baseline failure rates have been supplied. Numerical detector sensitivity/specificity and budget estimates would therefore be invented. Targets should follow a bounded baseline study, while the invariant safeguards above apply immediately.
