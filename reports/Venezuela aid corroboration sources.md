# Build reliable evidence for Venezuela aid

Research and planning · 7 November 2026

**Start with reliable intake, timestamped local checks and accountable fulfillment; add satellites as optional area-level context.** NASA Black Marble can help identify broad nighttime lighting disruptions, while rainfall and optical/radar observations describe environmental conditions. None verifies a household's electricity connection, piped-water access, drinking-water safety or individual need. Free imagery, free processing allowances, commercial nonprofit discounts and emergency disaster access have different eligibility and redistribution rules. The most useful first experiment combines independent local checks with a small NOAA-20 nighttime-light study, adding rainfall or reservoir analysis only where it informs a concrete operating decision. Reliability must be maintained throughout the system through definitions, provenance, corrections, freshness monitoring, independent validation and human accountability. **Conflicting evidence, missing reports or absent satellite signals must never automatically deny, downgrade or close assistance.**

This report builds on [PLATFORM_PLAN.md](../PLATFORM_PLAN.md) and the current [component architecture](../components/README.md), including the separate Python data worker and [data-job protocol](../components/contracts/data-jobs.md). It proposes changes, not a deployed integration. Public documentation and catalogs were researched; authenticated downloads, local coverage, actual latency and detector accuracy were not tested. No accounts were registered, providers contacted, services purchased or integrations deployed. Pilot communities, infrastructure boundaries, staffing capacity and independent reference observations remain unspecified.

## Local service evidence should lead the shortlist

**Rank 1: structured community intake, independent sentinel checks and authenticated utility notices.** Keep the existing distinction between electricity status, piped-water status, drinking-water reserves and requested support. Ask when a condition was actually observed, not merely when the message arrived. A fixed set of charging-site operators or community volunteers can record checks at agreed times, including normal periods. This creates a more useful comparison than asking only for confirmation after an alert. Such checks describe the location checked; they do not establish all nearby household conditions.

Utility notices are potentially strong operational evidence when their named circuit, pumping system or service zone matches the report. Store what the operator announced separately from what a resident or volunteer observed. A scheduled outage does not prove that it happened; an announcement of restoration does not prove water reached every tap. Authenticate the original publisher and capture revisions or cancellations. A forwarded screenshot with a familiar logo is insufficient provenance.

**No currently accessible official operational API, reliable publication cadence, complete historical archive or redistribution license for Corpoelec, Hidrocapital or Hidroven was verified in this research.** This is a verification gap, not evidence that official channels do not exist. The NGO should identify its actual local channels during operational design. Begin with manual, attributed evidence entry; do not make a utility integration a launch dependency or promise a live feed that has not been tested.

**Rank 2: NOAA-20 Black Marble in shadow mode for broad power events.** This is the strongest open satellite candidate for the power use case, particularly in ordinarily lit urban areas. It should help reviewers interpret an area episode, not score households. A critical current change is that NOAA permanently ceased Suomi-NPP data distribution on **2 November 2026 at 15:40 UTC**. New live monitoring must use NOAA-20/NOAA-21 products rather than assuming the familiar VNP46 stream continues. Historical Suomi-NPP imagery remains useful. ([NOAA cessation confirmation](https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/news/update-4-cessation-of-suomi-national-polar-orbiting-partnership-s-npp-data-users-november-02-2026-1540-utc-and-decommissioning-issued-november-2-2026-1800-utc))

**Rank 3: Internet-disruption signals as secondary clues.** IODA provides country, subnational and autonomous-system views with active probing, BGP and network-telescope signals, and documents a v2 API. Its Venezuela dashboard establishes country coverage; detailed geographic labels and completeness still need inspection. Cloudflare Radar offers outage records and a token-authenticated API. Its own analysis of the August 2024 Venezuela outage reported a large nationwide traffic decline, demonstrating relevance to major events rather than household-level sensitivity. ([IODA Venezuela](https://ioda.inetintel.cc.gatech.edu/country/VE?view=view2), [IODA API](https://api.ioda.inetintel.cc.gatech.edu/v2/), [Cloudflare outage API](https://developers.cloudflare.com/radar/investigate/outages/), [Cloudflare event analysis](https://blog.cloudflare.com/q3-2024-internet-disruption-summary/))

Internet failure can also reflect telecom equipment, routing, policy, usage changes or mobile-network behavior; backup-powered connectivity can persist during an electricity failure. ISP geography is not an electrical feeder boundary. Raw network measurements can provide independent evidence, while a provider's published causal annotation can depend on the same public reports being evaluated. Preserve these provenance paths separately. IODA's exact delay, oldest accessible history, license and SLA were not established. Cloudflare API/download data are CC BY-NC 4.0, while published graph/image downloads are CC BY 4.0; annotation delay and required historical retention remain POC questions. ([Cloudflare sources and licensing](https://radar.cloudflare.com/about))

**Rank 4: CHIRPS rainfall context and a supply-linked reservoir series.** These are useful when coordinators need to understand sustained catchment dryness or a visible reservoir change. They are weak tests of short piped-water interruptions. Only analyze a reservoir as supply context after confirming its relationship to the community's system. A nearby lake is not automatically the source of the local network.

**Rank 5: humanitarian reference data and periodic independent reports.** OCHA Common Operational Datasets supply administrative geography and codes useful for consistent joins. The current status page lists Venezuela administrative-boundary vintage 2021, population-statistics vintage 2011 and an ADM2 code mismatch. Preserve dataset and boundary versions; do not treat old population counts as a current denominator for household need. HDX resource-level licensing and files were not independently verified because the dataset page was inaccessible to the research tool. ([OCHA COD overview](https://ocha-dap.github.io/cod-site/), [COD status](https://ocha-dap.github.io/cod-site/status/))

ReliefWeb's JSON API provides humanitarian reports and metadata, but now requires a pre-approved `appname`; this research did not seek approval. Source organizations retain rights in their content, so API access is not blanket republication permission. CEDICE describes monitoring based on press, social media and citizen testimony; the inspected archive exposed 2020–2024 reports, not a verified current daily feed. UNDP's published Twitter-based service-indicator methodology demonstrates an approach, not an established live integration. These sources help contextualize patterns and reporting gaps, but can repeat the platform's own upstream claims. OVSP and VE Sin Filtro were not sufficiently verified for operational recommendation. ([ReliefWeb API](https://apidoc.reliefweb.int/), [CEDICE methodology and archive](https://cedice.org.ve/ogp/gasto-publico/servicios/), [UNDP methodology](https://www.undp.org/es/venezuela/publicaciones/indicadores-de-calidad-de-servicios-basicos-un-enfoque-en-twitter))

## Open satellite products measure conditions at different scales

### Nighttime lights offer a useful but incomplete power proxy

NASA Black Marble's standard suite includes A1 radiance, A2 corrected daily nighttime radiance, A3 monthly and A4 annual products. Its grid is **15 arc-seconds, approximately 500 m at the equator**. Global coverage includes Venezuela, subject to acquisition and quality gaps. Standard Collection 2 history starts in January 2012 for Suomi-NPP and January 2018 for NOAA-20. Published LANCE directories list NOAA-20 `VJ146A1_NRT`/`VJ146A2_NRT` and NOAA-21 `VJ246A1_NRT`/`VJ246A2_NRT`. Catalog existence verifies an access path, not a successful current Venezuela download. ([NASA suite](https://ladsweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/missions-and-measurements/science-domain/nighttime-lights), [LANCE product directory](https://nrt3.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/archive/allData/5200/))

Downloads are free, with Earthdata authentication for relevant archive access. NASA's general scientific-data policy provides open licensing unless specially marked; retain product-specific rights, DOI, version and attribution. Use LAADS API v2, since current alerts include retirement of the classic API. The Black Marble guide describes roughly three-hour near-real-time capability, but standard products can arrive substantially later. **No acquisition-to-first-available latency distribution was measured here.** Reprocessing and file modification times cannot substitute for that measurement. NRT retention and actual current tile completeness also require testing; use standard archives for reproducible historical analysis. ([NASA licensing](https://science.data.nasa.gov/about/license), [LAADS API v2](https://ladsweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/tools-and-services/api-v2/specs/), [LAADS alerts](https://ladsweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/alerts-and-issues), [Black Marble guide](https://ladsweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/api/v2/content/archives/Document%20Archive/Science%20Data%20Product%20Documentation/Black-Marble_v2.0_UG_2024.pdf))

Use current, quality-controlled observed radiance for outage analysis. Gap-filled values can come from earlier observations or climatology; a complete-looking image does not establish present conditions. Track cloud and quality flags, valid area, latest real observation and imputation separately. Satellite sampling occurs at particular nighttime overpasses: a short daytime outage can be entirely invisible. Persistent clouds, low rural lighting, streetlight schedules, generators, industrial flares, population movement and seasonal changes all complicate interpretation. A 500 m cell can include many households and several circuits. Bright surroundings cannot disprove a household outage, and dimming alone cannot establish its cause. ([Black Marble guide](https://ladsweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/api/v2/content/archives/Document%20Archive/Science%20Data%20Product%20Documentation/Black-Marble_v2.0_UG_2024.pdf), [NASA humanitarian guidance](https://nasalifelines.org/hfs-data-series/human-rights/))

Processing changes can imitate real events. NASA documented a calibration-coefficient problem that caused a spurious radiance decline and required reprocessing. Record collection and processing versions, monitor known issues, and retain the original basis of an earlier review when corrected data arrive. Start with a NOAA-20 baseline and forward stream; add NOAA-21 only after examining offsets rather than silently combining sensors. ([NASA known issues](https://landweb.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/knownissue?as=5200&sensor=VIIRS))

EOG monthly and annual VIIRS composites offer 15 arc-second history from 2012 covering Venezuela; their coverage counts help distinguish darkness from absent good observations. They are primarily historical context. Some EOG products are openly licensed, but the linked product-specific license document failed during review, and current nightly access costs, API guarantees and redistribution scope remain unresolved. Nightfire is a separate combustion product with licensing requirements, not a free replacement for electricity monitoring. NASA, EOG and visualization portals can all use the same VIIRS acquisition; they are not three independent witnesses. ([EOG VNL](https://eogdata.mines.edu/products/vnl/), [EOG Nightfire](https://eogdata.mines.edu/products/vnf/))

### Surface water and rainfall do not measure piped supply

**Sentinel-2** is the preferred conditional optical source. Visible/NIR bands have 10 m sampling, SWIR bands 20 m and atmospheric bands 60 m; nominal two-satellite revisit is five days at the equator. Global land coverage includes Venezuela. L2A global operations began in December 2018, with historical reprocessing extending back to 2015. Revisit is not cloud-free frequency. Inspect cloud masks over the reservoir itself and retain valid-area fractions; a low whole-scene cloud percentage can conceal the target. SWIR resampled to 10 m does not gain native 10 m information. ([Copernicus Sentinel-2](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/data-collections/copernicus-sentinel-missions/sentinel-2), [ESA product history](https://sentiwiki.copernicus.eu/web/s2-products))

**Sentinel-1 radar** works day/night through clouds and has an archive beginning in 2014. A satellite repeats its orbit in 12 days, with six-day repeat possible for a two-satellite configuration, but operational acquisition plans determine actual Venezuela opportunities. Its common IW high-resolution GRD product resolves roughly **20 × 22 m on a 10 m grid**. Clouds cease to be the principal obstacle, but radar shadow, buildings, vegetation, terrain and surface roughness complicate water classification. Radar is a second-stage option if optical cloud losses justify the specialist work. ([ESA mission](https://sentiwiki.copernicus.eu/web/s1-mission), [ESA products](https://sentiwiki.copernicus.eu/web/s1-products), [acquisition maps](https://asf.alaska.edu/daac/sentinel-1-acquisition-maps/), [UN-SPIDER mapping limitations](https://un-spider.org/advisory-support/recommended-practices/recommended-practice-flood-mapping/in-detail))

Copernicus Sentinel data are free with broad reuse, modification and redistribution rights, prescribed attribution and no fitness warranty. CDSE offers STAC, OData and S3 access plus processing services. Its documented expectation is Sentinel-1/2 availability within 24 hours of sensing, not a daily observation or guaranteed SLA. General-user processing and transfer quotas apply; some allowances are temporary, and offline assets require ordering. Verify current account limits before implementation. Local download and processing of a small approved area avoids assuming that a free imagery license includes unlimited hosted computation. ([Sentinel license](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/licences/ec-sentinel), [CDSE latency FAQ](https://documentation.dataspace.copernicus.eu/FAQ.html), [CDSE quotas](https://documentation.dataspace.copernicus.eu/Quotas.html))

**Landsat** provides a long worldwide archive and 30 m multispectral imagery, with 16-day repeat per satellite. Free downloads and documented machine/cloud access make it useful for baselines. Its Level-2 latency is product-specific: current USGS guidance describes approximately three days for Landsat 9, while Landsat 8 tiering can take 14–16 days before subsequent Level-2 availability. Do not use a generic “hours” claim to promise immediate water evidence. Imagery use and redistribution are unrestricted with credit requested; compute, storage and cloud charges are separate. ([USGS access](https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-data-access), [Level-2 production](https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-collection-2-level-2-science-products), [USGS cloud guide](https://d9-wret.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/assets/palladium/production/s3fs-public/atoms/files/LSDS-2032-Landsat-Commercial-Cloud-Direct-Access-Users-Guide-v2.pdf.pdf))

**JRC Global Surface Water v1.5**, released in October 2026, provides Landsat-derived 30 m history through 2024, beginning in 1984. GeoTIFF, Earth Engine and other access options make it useful for seasonal baselines, not live outages. Preserve attribution and release-specific caveats: collection changes introduce spatial offsets, newer history needs joining to older assets, and sparse observations affect recurrence. Colored WMTS visualizations are not substitutes for analytical pixel values. ([JRC access and release notes](https://global-surface-water.appspot.com/download))

**CHIRPS v3** supplies 0.05° rainfall grids, about 5.5 km north–south, over 60°N–60°S from 1981 to near present. Venezuela is included. Preliminary pentads arrive approximately two days after their end; final data normally arrive in the third week of the next month. Direct downloads include GeoTIFF/COG and NetCDF. Use pentad/monthly catchment anomalies for the first experiment. Daily values are disaggregated: the daily “sat” product uses IMERG Late, so agreement with IMERG is partly shared lineage. The provider uses public-domain and CC BY wording; retain attribution and the actual license statement with the downloaded version. CHIRPS v2 production is scheduled to end after December 2026; start new work on v3. ([CHIRPS v3](https://www.chc.ucsb.edu/data/chirps3), [v2 transition](https://chc.ucsb.edu/data/chirps))

**IMERG V07B** adds recent rainfall context at 0.1°, about 11 km, with half-hour estimates and reprocessed history to January 1998 for the relevant current archive. Early, Late and Final are revisions of the same phenomenon, with nominal latencies around four hours, 12–14 hours and 3.5 months respectively. Check the exact selected product because older access pages describe shorter historical ranges. Data are free through NASA archives and services including Earthdata/GES DISC, with authentication for some access methods; cite product/version and preserve revisions. No Venezuela gauge-density audit or actual delivery benchmark was performed. ([NASA current Early Run](https://gpm.nasa.gov/taxonomy/term/1357), [latency explanation](https://gpm.nasa.gov/resources/faq/what-determines-latency-imerg), [IMERG access](https://gpm.nasa.gov/data/imerg), [GPM data policy](https://gpm.nasa.gov/data/policy))

These products can establish visible water extent or estimated rainfall at their stated scales. Reservoir volume additionally requires a validated level/bathymetry relationship; tap supply requires functioning intakes, pumps, electricity, treatment and distribution. Full reservoirs and recent rain can coexist with dry taps. Water appearance cannot establish microbiological or chemical safety. Preserve the platform's supply-release/check records and household drinking-water assessment. Match rainfall to a real catchment and supply evidence to a documented service zone; administrative proximity alone is insufficient. **Environmental context normally receives `context_only`, not a household service confirmation.**

## Nonprofit access rarely means unrestricted free operations

**Open data comes first.** NASA, Sentinel, Landsat, JRC and CHIRPS are available to broader communities than nonprofits. Their zero imagery price does not eliminate analyst time, downloads, storage, processing, backups or maintenance. No per-area runtime or financial benchmark was performed, so a precise monthly operating estimate would be invented. For a small proof of concept, use bounded local processing and existing equipment before introducing a paid-cloud dependency.

**Earth Engine is a processing option with eligibility and quotas.** Google's current noncommercial tiers describe Community at 150 EECU-hours per month, Contributor at 1,000 with an active billing account, and Partner at 100,000 subject to an application and qualifying impact requirements. The quota rollout began in April 2026. EECU-hours are computation units, not elapsed runtime. Noncommercial Earth Engine access does not mean other Google Cloud services are free, nor does it override the rights of each dataset. An eligible, already approved project could be useful, but the pilot should not depend on a future approval or presume a quota will suffice. ([Google noncommercial tiers](https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/guides/noncommercial_tiers), [eligibility](https://earthengine.google.com/noncommercial/))

**Planet's nonprofit program is discounted commercial access, not a universal free grant.** Published offerings describe 12-month, noncommercial, single-entity bundles, with different archive and tasking allocations; dollar prices are not published on the program page. Single-entity scope is material to a platform serving multiple NGOs. Planet's free academic Basic program explicitly excludes nonprofit and government employees and carries a 30-day PlanetScope delay for eligible academic users; it is not an NGO production workaround. ([Planet nonprofit program](https://www.planet.com/nonprofit/), [academic eligibility and terms](https://www.planet.com/industries/education-and-research/))

Before any later procurement, verify Venezuela scene inventory and ordering restrictions, specific sensor resolution and delivery timing, API entitlement, raw-data versus derivative redistribution, vendor hosting, multi-NGO access, retention after expiry and processing/egress charges. None was established as an operational guarantee here. Higher-resolution optical imagery still suffers cloud and timing limits and cannot see a home's electrical connection or water safety. Commercial imagery should follow a demonstrated decision need, not precede it.

**Vantor, formerly Maxar, Open Data is event-triggered access.** Current documentation links disaster releases and an activation protocol describing selected major sudden-onset events. The linked protocol specifies noncommercial attribution licensing, but each event's actual terms should be checked. It is not an unrestricted global archive or a guaranteed Venezuela feed; no current Venezuela event inventory, fixed resolution or delivery SLA was verified. ([Vantor Open Data](https://xpress-docs.maxar.com/OpenData/OpenData_intro.htm), [linked activation protocol](https://maxar-marketing.s3.amazonaws.com/files/downloads/119757_opendataprotocol_2020_04.pdf))

**The International Charter is an emergency-response mechanism.** Venezuela appears on its authorized-country list, but direct access ordinarily involves a national disaster-management authority or delegated authorized agency with mapping capacity. An independent NGO is not automatically eligible to activate it. Downstream imagery conditions, activation timing and acquisition depend on the event and mechanism; this is not a scheduled monitoring API. NASA/ESA research-access programs were not verified sufficiently to recommend them for this NGO's operations. ([Charter universal access](https://upload.disasterscharter.org/universal-access), [Authorized User criteria](https://download.disasterscharter.org/become-au), [activation process](https://disasterscharter.org/charter-activation-process))

## Reliability requires a maintained lifecycle and explicit evidence model

### Define fitness for use rather than a single truth score

The useful dimensions are accuracy, validity, completeness, uniqueness, consistency and timeliness, with representativeness and source independence assessed separately. A syntactically valid answer can be inaccurate; an accurate sample can be unrepresentative; a reliable historical measurement can be too late for dispatch. The UK data-quality framework recommends critical-field definitions, metadata, ownership and root-cause improvement. HDX's review process similarly checks relevance, timeliness, interpretability and comparability. These principles support a maintained operating process rather than a global resident “trust” score. ([Government data-quality framework](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-government-data-quality-framework/the-government-data-quality-framework-guidance), [HDX quality process](https://centre.humdata.org/ufaqs/how-does-hdx-ensure-data-quality/))

At intake, test Spanish terminology with residents and keep unknown, approximate and not-applicable distinct. Preserve observation time, onset estimate, arrival time, timezone, location precision and firsthand/secondhand scope. Validate units and ranges without inventing missing facts. Confirm structured summaries; keep transcription or extraction as a draft until confirmed or reviewed. Missing GPS, documents or photos must not make a household ineligible. A correction should record actor, reason, changed fields and supersession while preserving an appropriately retained history.

Deterministic message replay detection is different from household matching. Repeated provider message IDs and fulfillment operation IDs should not create new facts. Shared phones, similar names and nearby coordinates should only create review candidates. Household merges require human review, reversible merge/split history and tenant-scoped constraints. Repeated requests in a new period can be legitimate. A service transition at a later time is not a contradiction.

Record lineage at the measurement and claim level. A WhatsApp forward, a news story and a monitoring report quoting it are one upstream account. Two satellite products can derive from one acquisition. CHIRPS daily “sat” and IMERG are partially dependent. Institutional independence does not guarantee independent measurement. Do not add votes or confidence percentages without a validated model of that dependence.

Reviewers should adjudicate a specific comparable claim, never label a person dishonest. Check phenomenon, subject, overlapping observation intervals, location uncertainty and service-zone compatibility before considering conflict. Preserve `not_comparable` and `unresolved` outcomes. Nearby households can use different feeders; a utility can announce restoration while a home remains disconnected. Urgent need and uncertain evidence can coexist. Restoration does not close a drinking-water need; a satellite light anomaly does not establish access to a charging site.

Sampling must include unflagged records, normal periods, low-reporting communities and assisted/offline channels. Otherwise quality estimates describe only what the system already notices. Double-review a small sample, retain initial disagreement and reconcile it. Evaluate models on independently collected reference observations and held-out dates or areas. Do not use reports that generated an alert as independent validation of that alert. WhatsApp participation is a convenience sample; fewer reports can indicate connectivity loss, not recovery.

Fulfillment needs its own reliability checks. Keep requested, approved, allocated, dispatched, delivered and recipient-confirmed quantities distinct. Reconcile water stock, returns and losses; reconcile charging slots, completions and interruptions. An unanswered follow-up is unknown confirmation, not successful or failed delivery. A distributor's completion record and a recipient's confirmation should remain separately attributable. Maintain an accessible correction/complaint route and audit whether corrections reach dashboards, exports and shared views.

### Add provenance-rich indicators without fabricating household observations

The existing `observation.assessment_id` is required. External rasters, rainfall and utility notices should therefore enter a separate external-evidence path, not invented assessments or households. The following logical additions extend the existing evidence/review model; they are not executable migrations.

```text
external_source(id, provider, product, version, access_basis,
  license_url, license_snapshot_ref, attribution, permitted_uses,
  sharing_scope, expected_cadence, source_owner, terms_checked_at)
source_lineage(id, source_id, upstream_source_id?, upstream_asset_id?,
  dependency_kind, evidence_reference, confidence_reason)
source_fetch(id, source_id, requested_window, first_seen_at?, retrieved_at?,
  status, cursor?, retry_count, failure_reason?, checksum?)
external_asset(id, source_id, provider_item_id, product_version,
  acquired_from, acquired_to, published_at?, first_seen_at, retrieved_at,
  footprint, crs, native_resolution, grid_spacing, quality_flags,
  source_url, checksum, artifact_reference, supersedes_asset_id?)
external_indicator(id, asset_id, area_id, boundary_version,
  phenomenon, value?, unit?, observation_interval, availability,
  valid_coverage_fraction, observed_or_imputed, last_valid_observation_at?,
  baseline_reference?, method_version, processing_version,
  uncertainty_description, limitations, review_after, supersedes_indicator_id?)
indicator_observation_link(id, organization_id, indicator_id, observation_id,
  relation, spatial_overlap, temporal_overlap, comparability_reason,
  rule_version, reviewer_membership_id?, reviewed_at?)
indicator_episode_link(id, organization_id, indicator_id, outage_episode_id,
  relation, reason, rule_version)
review_indicator_evidence(id, organization_id, review_decision_id, indicator_id)
data_quality_issue(id, organization_id?, issue_type, severity,
  affected_record_reference, owner, detected_at, status,
  root_cause?, correction_reference?, resolved_at?)
```

Use explicit indicator states for usable, partial, unavailable, stale, obscured and insufficient baseline; retain an unknown reason instead of a numerical zero. Distinguish nominal temporal resolution, actual acquisition, publication, first successful observation of availability, ingestion and review. `published_at` can be unknown; do not manufacture it from download time. Link relations should include `context_only`, `supports_area_condition`, `potentially_conflicts` and `not_comparable`. An area support relation must never be promoted automatically to household verification. Separate typed links avoid unchecked polymorphic identifiers.

Public licensed assets can be shared reference data, but household comparisons and reviewer decisions remain organization-owned. Commercial/private assets also require explicit entitlements and retention limits. Enforce organization-scoped relationships, authorization and artifact access on every path; an organization ID in an input is not authorization. Raw rasters belong in controlled artifact storage, not case rows or queue payloads. Keep household coordinates, phone numbers and WhatsApp content out of upstream satellite requests. OCHA's responsibility guidance applies to both personal and non-personal humanitarian data and supports defined sharing protocols, access controls and disclosure-risk review. ([OCHA data responsibility](https://centre.humdata.org/data-responsibility/))

The current component plan already places geospatial work in a **Python data worker**. Put approved-source adapters, quality decoding, bounded raster processing and lineage capture there. Use the existing versioned JSON job/results contracts and scoped internal API; the data worker gets no direct application-database access or authority to modify cases. The API validates provenance, artifact ownership, units, finite values and current lease tokens. The operational worker ingests accepted results idempotently and invokes shared domain comparability rules. Web evidence cards show source links, observation and retrieval times, usable coverage, baseline, limitations and review outcomes. Do not place remote processing on the WhatsApp webhook path. ([Data worker plan](../components/data-worker/README.md), [data-job contract](../components/contracts/data-jobs.md))

### Assign owners and monitor interpretable denominators

Caseworkers should own corrections and current needs; a data steward should own dictionaries, aliases, source rights and quality incidents; evidence reviewers should own dispositions; operators should own failed jobs and source freshness; coordinators should own fulfillment reconciliation. Proposed cadence is a short daily check of urgent unassigned cases, stale needs and failed feeds, plus a weekly quality review and periodic source/license revalidation. Tune the cadence to operating hours and capacity rather than creating unstaffed alerts.

Track observation-time completeness as records with a usable time or explicit uncertainty divided by all submitted observations. Track broad-location share against all observations, rather than hiding unmapped records. Report observation-to-arrival and arrival-to-review median/p90 separately, plus age of last confirmation for active needs. Measure valid source area-days divided by all monitored area-days, and distinguish source failure, clouds and insufficient baseline. Report false merges among reviewed merge decisions, independently checked claim agreement and unresolved share among checked claims, and reviewer disagreement among double-reviewed claims. These denominators prevent a neat percentage from concealing exclusions.

For fulfillment, report recipient confirmation among recorded completions, with contacted, unreachable, declined and pending counts shown separately. Track inventory/slot discrepancies and duplicate-operation rejections. For engineering, monitor rejected payloads, quarantined records, replay failures, expired leases and propagation of corrections. Stratify quality by area, channel and access barriers; do not reward staff for forced completeness or high corroboration rates. Each incident should record affected records, cause, repair, downstream correction and prevention, with restricted audit logs that avoid copying sensitive messages.

## A bounded shadow pilot can establish usefulness before integration

A proposed low-cost study uses two or three NGO communities, including a bright urban and dimmer/peripheral area, and at most one confirmed supplying reservoir/catchment. Use aggregate boundaries and a named reviewer. Run **six to eight weeks in shadow mode**, with a NOAA-20 baseline spanning at least six to twelve months and a longer seasonal water/rainfall baseline where needed. These are planning sizes, not claims of statistical adequacy. Keep assistance decisions on the normal human workflow throughout.

Begin by defining service terms, an observation schedule and independent reference checks. Authenticate the locally used utility channels and document their zones. Prepare historical NOAA-20 corrected observed radiance and inspect valid-pixel coverage; exclude or separately analyze industrial flares and near-zero baselines. Compare seasonally appropriate observations and show median, spread, valid count and time since last good retrieval. Prespecify any anomaly rule and evaluate it on held-out periods. Add CHIRPS pentad context only if it answers a coordination question; add Sentinel-2 only for a confirmed supply asset. Radar follows measured optical coverage failure, not the desire for more data.

Keep cash expenditure on new services at **$0 for this initial design**, using existing/local resources and approved public data access where available; account for staff hours, bandwidth, storage and maintenance explicitly. Registration or paid processing would require a separately authorized step. Set download-volume, runtime, storage and analyst-hour caps before execution. The purpose is to discover whether a useful, timely signal is affordable, not to assume that free imagery makes operations free.

Measure acquisition-to-first-availability and availability-to-ingestion separately where observable; report median/p90 and missing timestamps. Measure the fraction of area-days assessable, longest usable-observation gaps, false alerts per area-week, analyst minutes per useful alert and cost per monitored area-month. Precision/recall should be reported only against independently labeled area events, with sample counts and uncertainty, alongside an all-event view that includes events satellites could never observe. Stratify short daytime, persistent nighttime, cloudy, rural, partial and broad events. Do not claim household detection accuracy from aggregate pixels.

For water masks, evaluate visible-water classification against held-out human-reviewed imagery or safe field shoreline observations, with boundary uncertainty and terrain/vegetation strata. Do not score reservoir masks against tap availability: they measure different things. For operational usefulness, record whether evidence changed a follow-up, area investigation or dispatch-planning action and whether it arrived before that decision. A retrospective signal can be scientifically valid but operationally late. If evidence rarely arrives within the NGO's action window or consumes more review time than it saves, retain it for retrospective analysis rather than continuous operations.

Engineering safeguards can be required immediately: every accepted indicator must carry provenance, time, geography, rights and version metadata or an explicit quarantined/unknown reason; unavailable measurements must remain unknown; replay and lost completion responses must not duplicate evidence or fulfillment; stale leases and wrong-organization results must be rejected; reprocessing must preserve prior review bases; and no external result may directly change an assistance decision. Test source outages, delayed messages, stale maps, restored service with unmet need, shared phones, partial water deliveries and interrupted charging. These are concrete invariants. Detection thresholds and useful latency targets should be agreed only after measuring baseline coverage and reviewer capacity.

The strongest longer-term investment is a trustworthy evidence history that can accommodate better sources without changing the meaning of a household's need. A system that explains what was observed, where, when, by whom and with what limitations will remain useful when a satellite is retired, a provider changes terms or a new NGO joins. The pilot should earn each additional integration by demonstrating timely decision value while preserving the ability to help people whose circumstances remain difficult to observe.
